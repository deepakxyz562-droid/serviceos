## What is home services software?

Home services software is an all-in-one platform that helps businesses serving residential homeowners run their operations — the plumbers, HVAC contractors, electricians, landscapers, house cleaners, handymen, pest control, roofers, and the dozens of other trades that visit customer homes to deliver a service.

It combines:
- **Customer relationship management (CRM)** — customer profiles, service history, asset tracking
- **Scheduling and dispatch** — calendar, technician assignment, route optimization
- **Mobile field app** — job details, photo capture, signatures, on-site payment
- **Invoicing and payments** — line-item invoicing, SMS delivery, card/Apple Pay/ACH collection
- **Customer communication** — automated reminders, follow-ups, review requests
- **Increasingly, AI reception** — 24/7 phone and chat answering that captures leads after hours

The alternative is a stack of 5+ disconnected tools (a CRM, a scheduling app, a forms tool, a payments processor, a review collector, and Zapier to glue them together) costing $1,000+/month and breaking constantly. Good home services software replaces all of it in one platform.

## Home services software vs. field service software — what's the difference?

The terms overlap heavily. Here's the practical distinction:

- **Field service software** is the broader category — any business that sends technicians to customer locations, including commercial B2B services (industrial equipment repair, telecom installs, commercial HVAC).
- **Home services software** specifically targets businesses serving residential homeowners — plumbers, residential HVAC, house cleaners, landscapers, pest control. The workflows differ: recurring residential maintenance, after-hours emergencies, Google review collection for local SEO, and customer self-booking.

Most platforms serve both. Fieseros, Jobber, and Housecall Pro all fit both categories. ServiceTitan leans toward commercial and large residential fleets.

## What features matter most in home services software?

When evaluating platforms, prioritize these:

1. **24/7 phone answering** — Most home service businesses lose 30-50% of their leads to voicemail after hours. An AI receptionist captures them.
2. **Customer self-booking** — Let customers pick a slot from your real-time availability. Eliminates phone tag.
3. **Route optimization** — Re-orders the day's stops to minimize drive time. Fits 5 more jobs per tech per week.
4. **On-site payment collection** — Card, Apple Pay, ACH at the job. Don't leave without getting paid.
5. **Automated review requests** — Fire the moment a job completes. Climb local search rankings.
6. **Recurring maintenance automation** — Schedule once, auto-generate visits, invoices, and charges.
7. **Mobile app that works offline** — Techs work in basements and dead zones. Offline-first is essential.
8. **0% platform payment fees** — Read the fine print. Some tools charge 1-2% on top of the gateway fee.

## How to choose home services software

1. **Start with your biggest pain.** Missing calls? Prioritize a 24/7 AI receptionist. Cash flow problems? Prioritize on-site payment and 0% fees. Routing chaos? Prioritize route optimization.
2. **Calculate total cost of ownership.** Add the subscription + payment fees + add-ons + Zapier + your time managing it all. The cheapest subscription isn't always the cheapest platform.
3. **Test the mobile app yourself.** Have a tech use it for a week. If it's not fast and offline-capable, it'll fail in the field.
4. **Check migration support.** Can you import your existing customers and job history? If not, you're starting from scratch.
5. **Read the contract terms.** Annual contracts and per-seat pricing lock you in. Month-to-month, per-job pricing lets you scale on your terms.
