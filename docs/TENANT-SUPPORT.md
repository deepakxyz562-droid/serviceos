# Fieseros Tenant Guide — Social Publishing & Brand Brain

Welcome! This guide walks you through everything you need to publish posts to your social media accounts from Fieseros — and how to set up your Brand Brain so every AI-generated post sounds like *you*.

You don't need any technical skills to follow this guide. If you can use Facebook, you can use Fieseros Social Publishing.

## 1. Getting Started

Fieseros Social Publishing lets you publish to **six** social media platforms from a single dashboard:

- Facebook Page
- Instagram Business Account
- Google Business Profile
- LinkedIn
- Pinterest
- X (Twitter)

### What you can do

- **Publish now or schedule** posts to one or all six platforms at once.
- **Generate on-brand captions** with one click using Brand Brain + AI.
- **Track engagement** — likes, comments, shares, impressions — across all platforms in one analytics view.
- **Manage all your posts** — drafts, scheduled, published, and failed — in one list.

### Before you start

You'll need:

- A Fieseros account with a **Growth plan** or higher (for posting; Social Accounts view is available on all plans).
- Login credentials for each social platform you want to connect.
- For Facebook and Instagram: admin access to your Facebook Page.
- For Google Business Profile: a verified GBP location.
- For LinkedIn: your personal LinkedIn account (and admin access to a company page if you want to post as the company).

Ready? Let's start with your Brand Brain.

## 2. Setting Up Your Brand Brain

### What is Brand Brain?

Brand Brain is your AI assistant that learns your business voice. Once it's set up, **every AI-generated post, email, and message** Fieseros writes for you will match your brand style — same tone, same services highlighted, same call-to-action.

You only set it up once. After that, Brand Brain runs quietly in the background every time you click "AI Assist."

### How to set it up

1. In the left sidebar, go to **Setup & Admin → Brand Brain**.
2. Fill in your business details across the five sections:

   **Identity**
   - Business name (required)
   - Industry (required)
   - Website
   - Location / service area

   **Target Audience**
   - Who your ideal customer is (e.g., "Homeowners in Austin aged 30–55 looking for energy-efficient HVAC")

   **Brand Voice**
   - Tone: Professional, Friendly, Casual, Luxury, Playful, or Authoritative
   - Anything Fieseros should **never** say (forbidden phrases)

   **Offering**
   - Your key services
   - Your products
   - What makes you different (USPs)

   **Competitors**
   - Add competitor website URLs (helps the AI differentiate you)

3. **(Optional) Use AI Assist to auto-fill the form:**
   - Click the **AI Assist** button at the top of the form.
   - Enter your website URL.
   - Fieseros will fetch your homepage, analyze it, and pre-fill the form fields with suggested values.
   - Review the suggestions, edit anything that's wrong, and continue.

4. Review the live preview on the right — this shows exactly what context will be sent to the AI when generating content.

5. Click **Save**.

6. You'll see a confirmation toast, and a short **"Brand snapshot"** card appears on the right — this is the AI's 2–3 sentence summary of your brand.

That's it! Your Brand Brain is now active. Every time you click **AI Assist** in the Post Composer, it'll write captions in your voice.

> **Want to change it later?** Just go back to **Setup & Admin → Brand Brain**, edit any field, and click Save. New content will immediately use the updated profile.

## 3. Connecting Your Social Accounts

You only need to connect the platforms you actually use. There's no need to connect all six.

To get started:

1. In the left sidebar, go to **Content → Social Accounts**.
2. You'll see a grid of six platform cards. Each card shows a **Connect** button if it's not yet connected, or your account info if it is.

> **Tip:** Connecting uses a secure popup. If your browser blocks popups, allow popups for `fieseros.com` and try again.

### 3.1 Facebook Page

**Prerequisites:** You must be an **admin** of the Facebook Page you want to connect.

**Steps:**

1. Go to **Content → Social Accounts**.
2. Click **Connect** on the Facebook card.
3. A Facebook popup appears — log in with your Facebook account (if you aren't already).
4. Click **Continue** to grant Fieseros permission to publish to your Page.
5. Select which Facebook Page(s) to connect.
6. Done! Your Page is now connected and shows up as "Active" on the card.

**Troubleshooting:**

- **"No Pages found"** → You need to be an admin of at least one Facebook Page. Go to facebook.com, create or claim a Page, then reconnect.
- **"Permission denied"** → Reconnect and make sure you click "Continue" on the permissions screen (don't skip any).
- **"Token expired"** → Click **Reconnect** on the Facebook card and log in again. Facebook Page access tokens expire periodically; reconnecting refreshes them.

### 3.2 Instagram Business Account

**Prerequisites:**

- Your Instagram account must be a **Business** or **Creator** account (not personal).
- Your Instagram account must be **linked to a Facebook Page**.

**To convert to a Business account:**

1. Open the Instagram app on your phone.
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account**.
3. Choose **Business** and follow the prompts.

**To link to a Facebook Page:**

1. In the Instagram app, go to **Settings → Business → Linked Facebook Page**.
2. Select your Facebook Page.

**Steps:**

1. Go to **Content → Social Accounts**.
2. Click **Connect** on the Instagram card.
3. A Facebook popup appears (Instagram uses Facebook login) — log in.
4. Click **Continue** to grant permissions.
5. Select your Instagram Business account from the list.
6. Done! Your Instagram account is now connected.

**Troubleshooting:**

- **"No Business account found"** → Your Instagram isn't a Business account or isn't linked to a Facebook Page. See the prerequisites above.
- **"Wrong account showed up"** → You can connect multiple Instagram Business accounts — repeat the connect flow for each.

> **Note:** Instagram posts **require an image**. You won't be able to publish a text-only post to Instagram.

### 3.3 Google Business Profile

**Prerequisites:** Your Google Business Profile location must be **verified**. (If you see "Need verification" on Google's side, complete that first at https://business.google.com.)

**Steps:**

1. Go to **Content → Social Accounts**.
2. Click **Connect** on the Google Business Profile card.
3. A Google popup appears — log in with the Google account that manages your Business Profile.
4. Click **Continue** to grant permission.
5. Select the GBP location(s) you want to connect. Each location becomes a separate connection.
6. Done!

**What you can post to GBP:**

- **Standard posts** — text + image, like a social update.
- **Offer posts** — with a title, start/end date, and optional coupon code.
- **Event posts** — with a title, start time, and end time.

When composing a post, choose the GBP post type from the dropdown.

**Troubleshooting:**

- **"No locations found"** → Your Google Business Profile isn't verified yet, or you logged in with the wrong Google account.
- **Multiple locations** → Each location is a separate connection. Connect as many as you manage.

### 3.4 LinkedIn

**Prerequisites:** A LinkedIn account. If you want to post as a **company page**, you must be an admin of that page.

**Steps:**

1. Go to **Content → Social Accounts**.
2. Click **Connect** on the LinkedIn card.
3. A LinkedIn popup appears — log in.
4. Click **Continue** to grant Fieseros permission.
5. Fieseros will connect both:
   - Your **personal LinkedIn profile** (you can post on your own feed).
   - Any **company pages** you admin (you can post on the company's feed).
6. Done!

**Troubleshooting:**

- **"Company page missing"** → Make sure you're an admin of the page on LinkedIn. Editors and other roles don't have publishing access.
- **Posting to company page fails** → If Fieseros's LinkedIn Marketing Developer Platform review is still in progress, only personal-profile posts will work. Try posting to your personal profile first.

### 3.5 Pinterest

**Prerequisites:** A Pinterest account (business accounts recommended).

**Steps:**

1. Go to **Content → Social Accounts**.
2. Click **Connect** on the Pinterest card.
3. A Pinterest popup appears — log in.
4. Click **Continue** to grant permission.
5. Fieseros fetches your boards list.
6. Select a **default board** where your pins will be published (you can change this per-post in the composer).
7. Done!

**Troubleshooting:**

- **"No boards found"** → Create at least one board in Pinterest first.
- **Pin published but no image** → Pinterest requires images. The composer won't let you publish without one.

### 3.6 X (Twitter)

**Prerequisites:** An X (Twitter) account.

**Steps:**

1. Go to **Content → Social Accounts**.
2. Click **Connect** on the X (Twitter) card.
3. An X popup appears — log in.
4. Click **Authorize app** to grant Fieseros permission.
5. Done!

**Important — post limit:** The current Fieseros plan includes **10 posts per month** to X (Twitter). This is a shared limit across all your X posts. Once you hit the limit, X posts will fail until next month. Other platforms are not affected.

**Troubleshooting:**

- **"Rate limit exceeded"** → You've hit the 10 posts/month cap. Wait for next month or contact your plan administrator.
- **Image posts not yet supported** → Currently, X publishing is text-only. We're adding image support soon.

## 4. Creating Your First Post

Once your accounts are connected and your Brand Brain is set up, you're ready to publish!

1. Go to **Content → Create Post**.
2. **Select platforms:** check the box next to each platform you want to publish to. You can pick just one, or all six at once.

3. **Write your caption:**
   - Type directly in the caption box, OR
   - Click **AI Assist** — Brand Brain will generate an on-brand caption automatically. You can edit it before publishing.
   - The character counter shows the tightest limit among your selected platforms (e.g., 280 chars if X is selected).

4. **Add an image:**
   - Click the image upload area and choose a file (JPG or PNG).
   - **Required for Instagram and Pinterest.** Optional for the others.
   - Recommended size: 1080 × 1080 pixels (square).

5. **(Optional) Add a link:**
   - Available for Facebook, LinkedIn, and Google Business Profile.
   - Paste the URL in the Link field.

6. **(For Google Business Profile only)** Choose the post type:
   - **Standard** — text + image update.
   - **Offer** — fill in title, start date, end date, optional coupon code.
   - **Event** — fill in title, start time, end time.

7. **(For Pinterest only)** Select which board to pin to.

8. **Choose when to publish:**
   - **Publish now** — posts go out immediately.
   - **Schedule for later** — pick a date and time (see §5).

9. Click **Publish** (or **Schedule**).

10. You'll see a success toast, and the post appears in **Content → Posts** with a "Published" (or "Scheduled") status.

> **Mixed-platform tip:** Each platform gets its own version of the post automatically. Fieseros handles the differences (e.g., trimming the caption to 280 chars for X, attaching the image separately for Instagram) so you don't have to create multiple posts.

## 5. Scheduling Posts

Scheduling lets you write a post now and have it publish automatically at a future date and time.

### How to schedule

1. In the Post Composer, select **Schedule for later** instead of **Publish now**.
2. Pick a date and time.
3. Click **Schedule**.

The post is saved with a "Scheduled" status and will publish automatically at the chosen time.

### How the scheduler works

- Fieseros checks for due scheduled posts **every minute**.
- When the scheduled time arrives, the post is published to each selected platform.
- If a publish fails (e.g., a token expired between scheduling and publishing), you'll see the error in the Posts list and can retry.

> **Tip:** Schedule posts during your audience's peak engagement hours — typically 9–11 AM and 7–9 PM in your audience's time zone.

### How to cancel a scheduled post

1. Go to **Content → Posts**.
2. Filter by status: **Scheduled**.
3. Find the post you want to cancel.
4. Click the **Cancel** button (or Delete).
5. The post moves to "Cancelled" status and will NOT be published.

> Once a scheduled post is published, you can no longer cancel it. You can only delete it (which also deletes it from the social platform, where supported).

## 6. Managing Published Posts

All your posts — drafts, scheduled, published, and failed — live in one place.

### View all posts

1. Go to **Content → Posts**.
2. You'll see a table (desktop) or card list (mobile) of all your posts.

### Filter and search

Use the filter bar at the top:

- **Status** — Draft, Scheduled, Published, Failed, Cancelled
- **Platform** — show posts that include a specific platform
- **Date range** — last 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or custom
- **Search** — search by caption text

### Actions on a post

Click any post to open the detail view. From there you can:

- **Edit draft** — change caption, image, platforms (only available for Drafts).
- **Cancel scheduled** — prevents publication (only for Scheduled).
- **Retry failed** — re-attempts publishing to platforms that failed (only for Failed).
- **Delete** — removes the post from Fieseros. For published posts, this also attempts to delete the post from each social platform (where supported by the platform's API).

### Per-platform results

In the detail view, you'll see per-platform publish results — including:

- The external post URL (where available)
- Likes, comments, shares, impressions (from the latest metrics fetch)
- Error messages (for failed platforms)

## 7. Understanding Analytics

The Analytics page shows how your posts are performing across all platforms.

### Open analytics

Go to **Content → Analytics**. You'll see:

### KPI cards (top)

- **Posts** — total posts published in the selected period.
- **Impressions** — how many times your posts were seen.
- **Engagements** — total likes + comments + shares.
- **Engagement Rate** — engagements divided by impressions (a %).
- **Clicks** — link clicks (where reported by the platform).

### Platform breakdown

A bar chart showing impressions (or engagements) per platform — so you can see which platforms are pulling their weight.

### Trend over time

A line chart of daily impressions and engagements across the selected period. Spot spikes and slumps.

### Top performing posts

A list of your highest-engagement posts in the period. Click any to view the full detail.

### Date range

Switch between 7 days, 30 days, or 90 days at the top right.

### Important notes about metrics

- Metrics update **every 15 minutes** automatically. You don't need to refresh.
- Some platforms (especially Facebook and Instagram) have a **24–48 hour lag** before reporting final metrics. Don't be alarmed if numbers climb slightly over the first day after publishing.
- "Clicks" are only reported by some platforms (Facebook, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile). For platforms that don't report clicks (X, Pinterest), the Clicks KPI may show as 0 or be lower than expected.

## 8. Troubleshooting

### Common issues

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| **"Publish failed"** | Click the post in **Content → Posts** to see the per-platform error. Most common cause: an expired token. Click **Reconnect** on the affected account in Social Accounts, then click **Retry** on the post. |
| **"Image required"** | Instagram and Pinterest require an image. Add one to your post and try again. |
| **"Caption too long"** | Different platforms have different limits. Instagram: 2,200 chars max. X (Twitter): 280 chars max. Shorten your caption or remove X from the publish targets. |
| **"Token expired"** | The connection to the platform has expired. Go to **Content → Social Accounts**, click **Reconnect** on the affected card, and log in again. |
| **"Platform not available"** | The platform may be disabled on your plan or hidden by your plan administrator. Contact the person who manages your Fieseros subscription. |
| **"Rate limit exceeded" (X)** | You've hit the 10 posts/month X limit on your current plan. Wait until next month or contact your plan administrator. |
| **"AI Assist didn't generate anything"** | Make sure your Brand Brain is set up (**Setup & Admin → Brand Brain**). If it is, try again — AI generation can occasionally time out. |
| **Scheduled post didn't publish** | Check that the post's status is "Scheduled" and the time has actually passed. If both are true and it still didn't publish, the cron scheduler may have hit an error — contact support. |
| **Metrics not updating** | Metrics refresh every 15 minutes. If it's been more than an hour with no update, contact support. Some platforms also have a 24–48hr reporting lag. |
| **Pinterest pin has no image** | Pinterest requires images. Re-create the post with an image attached. |
| **LinkedIn company page publish fails** | This may happen if Fieseros is still in LinkedIn's review process. Personal-profile posts should still work. |

### Reconnecting an account

If a platform shows "Expired" or "Inactive":

1. Go to **Content → Social Accounts**.
2. Find the affected card.
3. Click **Reconnect**.
4. Log in to the platform and grant permissions again.
5. The card should return to "Active" status.

Reconnecting doesn't delete your past posts or analytics — it just refreshes the access token.

### Still stuck?

If you can't resolve an issue using this guide:

1. Take a screenshot of the error message.
2. Note the post ID (visible in the post detail view URL).
3. Note the platform and approximate time the error occurred.
4. Contact your Fieseros plan administrator or support.

## 9. FAQ

### Can I publish to all 6 platforms at once?

**Yes!** Just check all six platform boxes in the Post Composer. Fieseros will publish to each one automatically, handling the per-platform differences (caption length, image requirements, etc.) for you.

### Can I schedule posts?

**Yes.** You can schedule a post for any future date and time. The scheduler checks every minute, so your post goes out within 60 seconds of the scheduled time.

### Do I need to connect all platforms?

**No.** Connect only the platforms you actually use. The others will remain hidden in the composer until you connect them.

### Is there a post limit?

- **X (Twitter):** 10 posts per month on the current plan.
- **Other platforms:** no per-month limit.
- **Total posts:** governed by your Fieseros subscription plan (Growth plan and above).

### Can I edit a published post?

**No.** Once a post is published to a social platform, it can't be edited through Fieseros. You can:

- **Delete it** — which removes it from Fieseros AND from the social platform (where supported by the platform's API).
- **Edit the original on the platform directly** (e.g., edit a Facebook post on facebook.com) — but the changes won't sync back to Fieseros.

Drafts and scheduled posts CAN be edited before they're published.

### How often do metrics update?

**Every 15 minutes.** Some platforms have a 24–48 hour reporting lag, so numbers may rise slightly during the first day after publishing.

### Can I publish the same post multiple times?

Yes, but be careful — social platforms may flag duplicate content. Best practice: create a new post (or duplicate an existing draft) and tweak the caption before re-publishing.

### What image formats are supported?

JPG and PNG. Recommended size: 1080 × 1080 pixels (square). Maximum file size: 8 MB.

### Can I post a video?

Not yet. Video publishing is on our roadmap. For now, posts are text + single image.

### Can I publish to multiple Facebook Pages or LinkedIn company pages?

**Yes.** Connect each Page/company as a separate account in Social Accounts. In the Post Composer, check the box next to each one you want to publish to.

### What happens if I disconnect an account?

Disconnecting an account:

- Does NOT delete your past posts or analytics.
- Stops you from publishing new posts to that platform.
- The account's row remains in Fieseros for historical analytics.

To disconnect, click the **Disconnect** (or trash) button on the platform card in Social Accounts.

### Why does Instagram use Facebook login?

Instagram is owned by Meta (Facebook's parent company). Instagram Business accounts require a linked Facebook Page, and publishing uses the Facebook Graph API. So when you connect Instagram, you log in with Facebook — that's normal.

### Can my team members see and publish to my connected accounts?

Yes — anyone on your Fieseros tenant with the right permissions can see and use the connected social accounts. They don't need to log in to each platform individually.

### Can I use Brand Brain without connecting social accounts?

**Yes.** Brand Brain is also used for AI-generated message templates, WhatsApp templates, SMS replies, and chat replies. You can set up Brand Brain and use it for those features even if you never connect a single social account.
